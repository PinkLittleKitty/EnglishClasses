This lesson is mostly a reinforcement on previous topics, the main focus being understanding the difference between Present Perfect, and Past Simple.

### Present Perfect vs. Past Simple
This lesson focuses on understanding when to use each tense.

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Unfinished actions** that started in the past and continue now. <br> *"I've known Julie for ten years."* | **Finished actions** with no connection to the present. <br> *"I knew Julie for ten years (but we lost touch)."* |
| **Life experiences** (person is still alive). <br> *"My brother has been to Mexico."* | **Finished actions in the past** (person may be dead). <br> *"My grandmother went to Mexico."* |
| **Result in the present**. <br> *"I've lost my keys!"* (I can't get in now). | **No result in the present**. <br> *"I lost my keys yesterday"* (but I found them later). |
| **Unfinished time word** (*this week, today*). <br> *"I've seen John today."* | **Finished time word** (*yesterday, last week*). <br> *"I saw John last week."* |
