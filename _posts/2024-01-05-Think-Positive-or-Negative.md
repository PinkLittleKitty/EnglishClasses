### Future: Will vs. Shall
These auxiliaries are used to talk about the future, make promises, and offer suggestions.

<div class="grammar-box" markdown="1">
#### Will: Conjugation
| Type | Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **(+) Affirmative** | I will be (I’ll be) | We will be (We’ll be) |
| **(-) Negative** | I will not be (I won’t be) | We will not be (We won’t be) |
| **(?) Question** | Will I be? | Will we be? |
</div>

---

### Usos de "Will"
<div class="grammar-box" markdown="1">
1.  **Describir el futuro**: *"We’ll be at the hotel until 8pm."*
2.  **Predicciones**: *"The population will grow a lot."*
3.  **Decisiones espontáneas**: *"I’ll answer it" (al sonar el teléfono).*
4.  **Solicitudes**: *"Will you bring some water please?"*
5.  **Promesas y ofertas**: *"I’ll call you when I get home."*
6.  **Consecuencia condicional**: *"If it rains, I’ll take my umbrella."*
</div>

---

### Shall
**Shall** is more formal and is mostly used with "I" and "We" for offers and suggestions.

<div class="grammar-box" markdown="1">
#### Shall: Conjugation
| Type | Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **(+) Affirmative** | I shall help | We shall help |
| **(-) Negative** | I shall not help (I shan’t) | We shall not help (We shan’t) |
| **(?) Question** | Shall I help? | Shall we help? |
</div>

---

### Usos de "Shall"
<div class="grammar-box" markdown="1">
1.  **Ofertas (con I/We)**: *"Shall I make some lunch?"*
2.  **Sugerencias (con I/We)**: *"Shall we go to the park?"*
3.  **Obligaciones formales**: *"Applicants shall provide evidence."*
4.  **Promesas formales**: *"I shall never forget your help."*
5.  **Futuro formal**: *"We shall overcome this moment."*
</div>

> [!TIP]
> **Regla General**: Usa **Will** para casi todo en el futuro. Reserva **Shall** (en forma de pregunta) para ofrecer ayuda o sugerir planes con "I" o "We".
